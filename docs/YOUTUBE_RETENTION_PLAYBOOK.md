**Channel Blueprint: Existential Horror Anthology**

## 1. Executive Summary & Algorithmic Reality

On YouTube and TikTok, algorithmic distribution is not determined by views or likes—it is governed almost entirely by **Viewed vs. Swiped Away (VVSA)** and **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** on short-form, and **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** and **Average View Duration (AVD)** on long-form.

Horror is one of the highest-potential genres on video platforms because fear is an involuntary evolutionary reflex. However, 90% of horror creators fail because they rely on slow build-ups or predictable jump scares that cause instant viewer drop-off.

This playbook outlines the exact mechanics to achieve **>85% VVSA** and **>110% APV** on short-form, and **>55% AVD** on long-form.

## 2. Short-Form Mechanics (YouTube Shorts & TikTok: 30–45s)

### A. The 0.0s – 1.5s "Scroll-Stopper" Hook
In the feed, a viewer decides whether to swipe within **800 milliseconds**.
- **Rule 1: No Title Cards, No Intros**: Never start with "Welcome back" or a production logo.
- **Rule 2: Kinetic Sensory Disequilibrium**: The first frame must present an immediate contradiction or sensory tension:
  - *Example*: A girl blowing out a candle whose flame does not extinguish, or a character staring at a reflection that isn't mirroring them.
- **Rule 3: Audio Punch at Frame 1**: A subtle sub-bass thump (40Hz) or immediate atmospheric texture grabs headphone listeners instantly.

### B. The Pacing Rhythm (The "Micro-Climax" Curve)
To maintain 100% retention through a 30-second video:
1. **0.0s – 3.5s (The Spark)**: Relatable premise introduced. The viewer thinks they know what is happening.
2. **3.5s – 8.0s (The Malicious Grant)**: The first cognitive rupture (reality stops, sound cuts). Retention spikes due to curiosity.
3. **8.0s – 18.0s (The Visceral Escalation)**: The situation turns tactile and grotesque. Disgust activates the insular cortex, preventing swiping.
4. **18.0s – 25.0s (The Climax of Sensory Overload)**: Peak tension, rapid edits, hyper-tactile foley (chewing, enamel grinding, tendons snapping).
5. **25.0s – 30.0s (The Infinite Trap Reset)**: The sudden reversal that resets the loop.

```
Retention Tension Curve:
100% |  Hook (0s)      Rupture (3.5s)      Sensory Decay (15s)    Climax (24s)    Reset (26s) -> Looped!
     |   *----------------*--------------------*--------------------*----------------*
     |
 50% |
     +----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------> Time (30s)
```

### C. The 110%+ Retention Hack: The Infinite Seamless Loop
The secret to viral Shorts/TikTok reach is tricking the platform into recording **re-watches**.
- If a viewer finishes your 30s video and watches the first 5 seconds of the second loop before realizing it restarted, YouTube registers **116% Average Percentage Viewed**.
- **How to Engineer the Loop Seam**:
  - **Camera Match**: Shot 06's final frame must match Shot 01's starting frame in camera angle, subject position, and focal length.
  - **Audio Seam**: The foley cue of the reset (e.g. sharp intake of breath or candle flick) must seamlessly lead into the room tone of Shot 01.
  - **Dialogue / Monologue Circularity**: If there is internal monologue, the last sentence must finish the first sentence:
    - *Start*: "I only wanted one thing on my birthday..."
    - *End*: "...and now I have to keep wishing that..." (cuts back to start: "I only wanted one thing on my birthday...").

## 3. Long-Form Mechanics (YouTube 3–8 Minutes)

### The "Anthology Triptych" Structure
Directly turning a 30-second loop into an 8-minute slow-burn often results in flat pacing. Instead, use the **Anthology Triptych**:
- **Structure**: Package **3 distinct intrusive-thought loops** into a single 6-to-8 minute episode.
- **Narrative Frame**:
  - A clinical observer, an analog broadcast interruption, or a medical psychological examination reviewing "Subject 01, Subject 02, Subject 03".
  - Each micro-story (2 minutes each) follows the Four-Part Engine: Spark $\rightarrow$ Grant $\rightarrow$ Decay $\rightarrow$ Trap.
- **The "Retention Reset" Every 45 Seconds**:
  - Introduce an unexpected sensory pivot or foley silence every 45 seconds to re-hook wandering attention.

## 4. Audio-First Psychology: The Sound Design Engine

Audio accounts for **70% of perceived terror**. Visuals show the event; audio penetrates the subconscious.

| Acoustic Technique | Neurological Trigger | Application in Anthology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acoustic Vacuum** | Predatory threat detection | Sudden total dropout of room reverberation when time freezes. |

## 5. Metadata, Packaging & Thumbnail Secrets

### Thumbnails
- **Rule**: Never show a monster. Show **an ordinary situation corrupted by an unnatural mechanical detail**.
- *Example for The Birthday Loop*: Maya sitting alone under a single overhead tungsten bulb, birthday hat on, mouth smeared with thick white frosting, her unblinking eyes staring directly through the viewer, holding a bloody silver fork.
- **Color Palette**: Desaturated charcoal background with one high-contrast accent (the pastel yellow sweater and white frosting).
- **Text**: Max 2–3 words. Avoid cliché horror fonts (no dripping blood fonts). Use clean, industrial, clinical sans-serif (e.g., DIN, Helvetica, Courier Prime). Examples: `"SHE DIDN'T SHARE"`, `"THE LAST BITE"`, `"CYCLE 417"`.

### Titles
- ❌ Bad (Cliché): *"SCARY HORROR ANIMATION - Maya's Birthday Nightmare (CREEPY)"*
- ✅ Algorithm Winner: *"She wished she didn't have to share her cake."*
- ✅ Anthology Winner: *"3 Everyday Thoughts You Should Never Entertained (Animated Horror)"*
